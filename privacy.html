Privacy Policy

Amazon Order Tracker — last updated October 7, 2026

Amazon Order Tracker is a personal tool used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): only messages from amazon.com, to read order numbers, shipment amounts, item counts, delivery dates and statuses. It cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • Google Sheets: to write those details into a spreadsheet owned by the same account.

Where data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stored there and in the owner's own Google Sheet. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or AI model training.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the tool can no longer read mail or write to Sheets.
